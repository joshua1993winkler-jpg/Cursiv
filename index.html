Installation

Install in Three Steps

Windows 10 or 11. About 5 minutes including the AI download.

1
Download the installer

Click the Download for Windows button above — the file downloads directly.

If Windows SmartScreen appears, click More info then Run anyway. The file is safe — SmartScreen shows this for any new unsigned app.

2
Run the installer

Double-click the downloaded file and follow the prompts. The installer registers cursiv as a terminal command automatically.

After install, a browser window opens asking you to download Ollama (the local AI engine). This is a one-time 4–5 GB download. You only need to do it once.

3
Open a terminal and type cursiv

Open PowerShell, Command Prompt, or Windows Terminal — then type:

PowerShell
PS C:\Users\you> cursiv
𓂀 CURSIV v3.14-U09
Offline AI workspace | JW Architect Software
Type a message to begin. 'help' for commands.
𓂀 _

That's it. You're in.

If Something Goes Wrong

Common questions

?
"cursiv" is not recognized as a command

Close your terminal and open a new one — the PATH update only takes effect in new windows. If it still doesn't work, restart your computer once. The installer adds C:\Program Files\Cursiv to your system PATH.

?
The AI says it can't connect

Cursiv uses Ollama as its local AI engine. If you skipped the Ollama install step, download it from ollama.com then run ollama pull llama3.1 in a terminal. That's the one-time model download (~4.7 GB).

?
Bible verses say "not yet cached" or won't load

Each verse is fetched from the internet once, then stored locally forever. Make sure you're connected on first use. After that it works completely offline. Hardcoded passages (Proverbs, Psalms, Romans, 1 Corinthians, etc.) always work offline immediately.

?
Windows says the file is unsafe

Click More info, then Run anyway. Windows SmartScreen shows this for any app that doesn't have a code-signing certificate yet. The installer is safe — it only installs to C:\Program Files\Cursiv and adds a PATH entry.

?
I want to use Claude or GPT-4 instead of Ollama

Cursiv works without any API keys — Ollama is always available offline. To add external providers, type help inside Cursiv and look for the API key setup instructions. Once added, use hey claude or hey grok to route to them directly.

Why This Exists

The origin

"You cannot copy the output without going through the origin.
And the origin is the part I would not wish on anyone to have to understand from lived experience."

Cursiv did not begin with AI or code. It began with a man rebuilding himself from the inside out.

Joshua Winkler lives with schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, PTSD, and depression — not as managed background conditions but as active participants in how his brain works every day. He spent years in an ongoing war with psychosis: what it does to the architecture of thought, how it rewires the line between signal and noise, how it floods the system until it can no longer distinguish what is real from what it has constructed.

At one point that war pushed him to catastrophic overload. He attempted suicide. Not a metaphor — a real event, with a real aftermath, that he has never hidden. He is on medication for life: not because someone told him to be, but because he made that decision with open eyes and will not find out what happens without it.

In the years since, he rebuilt himself from raw experience — not from frameworks handed to him by someone who had never been there. Before writing a line of code, he built the Human Frontier Academy: a series of physical education decks covering 5,000 years of civilization history — eight decks, 200+ cards, one guide each — civilizations from Mesopotamia to the Technology Age. Then came the Eternal Pyramid: a structured thinking framework for surviving collapse — built to hold when everything else stops working.

He started using AI to help with this work. He came to see the major platforms as unwilling to give back what they had taken — his history, his data, the shape of his thinking. He requested it back. He received nothing. That loss became part of the story.

Cursiv is what happened when all of that — the physical systems, the philosophy, the hard internal work, the anger at what was taken — was finally given a living digital form. Every guardrail in this system exists for a reason. The privacy-first design, the local inference, the sovereignty layer — they are not aesthetic choices. They are the direct translation of hard-won internal architecture into software.

His foundational belief: deep empathy is not a soft idea. It is the first step toward a civilization that can look at itself honestly — that builds the best environment for all its people, not just those loud enough to be heard. Some are quiet. That silence deserves as much weight as the loudest voice in the room.

The deeper layers of Cursiv are designed to "undo you" — to reflect the architecture of a mind that has actually been taken apart and rebuilt. For those who go all the way through, they will come out the other side seeing the world in a light they may not have thought was possible. The guardrails are in place for the ones who cannot help themselves.
